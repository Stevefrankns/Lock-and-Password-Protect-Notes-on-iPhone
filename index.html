Before You Start: Compatibility & Requirements

Securing your data on iOS is generally seamless, but the specific steps to password protect notes on your iPhone depend heavily on your iOS version and iCloud settings.

Tested iOS Versions

The instructions in this guide have been verified on iOS 16, iOS 17, and iOS 18. If you are running iOS 15 or older, you will only have the option to use a custom Notes password (not your iPhone passcode).

Important Note on iCloud: If your notes sync across devices via iCloud, locking a note on your iPhone will also lock it on your Mac and iPad. Ensure your devices are logged into the same Apple ID and have Keychain enabled.

How to Lock Notes on iPhone (Native Method)

Apple provides a built-in feature to lock individual notes. This is the best approach if you only need to secure basic text and are staying entirely within the Apple ecosystem.

Method 1: Use the Native "Lock Note" Feature

Very Easy (1 min)
  1. Open the Notes app on your iPhone.
  2. Locate the note you want to secure and open it.
  3. Tap the More button (...) in the top right corner.
  4. Tap Lock (indicated by a padlock icon).
  5. Choose whether to use your iPhone Passcode or create a separate Custom Password.
  6. If prompted, authenticate using Face ID, Touch ID, or your chosen password.
What this method is best for:

Securing simple text lists, journals, or quick passwords natively on your device without downloading extra software.

Limitations:

You cannot lock an entire folder of notes at once native to the app—you must lock them individually. Furthermore, Apple Notes is notoriously difficult to access securely from a Windows PC or Android device, and it limits the types of attachments you can lock.

Lock a Notes Folder on iPhone vs Individual Notes

A frequent point of confusion is the difference between locking an individual note and attempting to lock a whole folder. Currently, iOS does not allow you to lock a folder within the Apple Notes app.

If you search for "how to lock a notes folder on iphone", the reality is you have to open that folder and manually lock each note inside it one by one. This is a primary reason many users migrate to third-party vault applications when dealing with dozens of secure documents.

Face ID vs Touch ID Security Models

When you set up notes protection, iOS allows you to link the lock to your biometrics. While incredibly convenient, understand the security trade-off:

  • Convenience: You don't have to type a master password in public.
  • Risk: Anyone who knows your primary iPhone device passcode can usually bypass Face ID/Touch ID fallback prompts. If you share your iPad or iPhone passcode with a family member, they can unlock your notes.

To prevent this, iOS allows you to set a Custom Notes Password that is completely separate from your device unlock code.

Reset Notes Lock Password on iPhone

If you search for "iphone notes lock forgot password" or "how to access locked notes on iphone forgotten password", here is the strict, owner-safe protocol provided by Apple:

The hard truth about forgotten Apple Notes passwords:

If you used a Custom Password and forgot it, Apple cannot recover the data for you. The encryption is end-to-end.

How to reset it for FUTURE notes:

  1. Go to Settings > Notes > Password.
  2. Tap Reset Password.
  3. Enter your Apple ID password to verify ownership.
  4. Create a new password.

Note: Resetting the password does not unlock your previously locked notes. They will retain the old password. The new password will only apply to notes you lock from this point forward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I Password Protect Notes on My iPhone?

Yes. You can protect individual notes using the native Apple Notes app by tapping the "..." icon and selecting "Lock." You can authenticate using Face ID, Touch ID, or a custom password.

How Do I Password Protect Notes on iPhone without Face ID?

If you prefer not to use biometrics, go to Settings > Notes > Password and select "Use Custom Password." Ensure the toggle for "Use Face ID" is turned off. You will now be required to type your password manually every time.

What is the difference between locking a note and locking a folder in iPhone Notes?

Apple Notes natively allows you to lock individual notes, but it does not currently support locking an entire folder at once. If you need folder-level security, you must use a third-party application.

How to access a locked note on iPhone if I forgot my password?

If you used your device passcode, simply enter the PIN you use to unlock your phone. If you created a custom Notes password and forgot it, the data cannot be recovered. You can reset the password in Settings to lock future notes, but old notes remain tied to the old password.

What happens to locked notes when I get a new iPhone?

If your notes are synced to iCloud, they will transfer to your new iPhone. You will need to enter the original custom password you set (or your new device's passcode if tied to device passcode) to open them.
